**Action:** Type your second search: "Dark and moody aesthetic" (or similar). Click Search.
**Speak:** "What makes this powerful is that it understands concepts, not just objects. If I search for 'dark and moody aesthetic', the model maps the abstract text vector to the image vectors, returning results based on the lighting and feeling of the image."
